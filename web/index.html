ParkGlas

Find parking across Ireland

A free iPhone app with over 6,000 car parks on one map, live free-space counts for Cork city, fuel stations and EV chargers. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Park me now picks an open car park near you in one tap. Save where you parked, set your ticket time and get a reminder before it runs out.
