Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

In short: ParkGlas has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect any personal data. What you save in the app stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

ParkGlas is published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}. You can reach us at {{CONTACT}}.

Your location

If you allow it, ParkGlas uses your location while the app is open to show parking near you and to offer a timer when you appear to have parked. Your location is used on your iPhone only. It is never sent to us.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your home city, display and reminder settings, and your preferred map app.
  • Your car's name, fuel type and charging connectors, if you enter them.
  • Your favourite car parks.
  • Your saved parking spot: its location, your note, your photo and your ticket time.
  • Problems you report about a car park or charger. In this version, reports stay on your iPhone.

All of this is deleted when you delete the app. We have no copy.

Services the app contacts

  • Apple Maps. The map, search suggestions and street imagery come from Apple. Apple receives the area of the map you look at and what you search for, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Cork City Council open data. Live free-space counts are fetched from the council's public data service. The request contains no information about you; like any website, the service can see your internet address.
  • Our map data file. The app may download an updated list of car parks from this website, which is hosted by GitHub. The request contains no information about you; GitHub can see your internet address.
  • Other map apps. When you tap Waze, Apple Maps or Google Maps, ParkGlas opens that app with the car park's location. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Notifications

Ticket reminders are created and shown by your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Children

ParkGlas is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version shares community reports between drivers or changes anything above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
