Where does ParkGlas work?

The Republic of Ireland. Car parks, fuel stations and chargers outside it are not shown.

Why do only some car parks show free spaces?

Live counts come from open data published by councils. Today that is Cork city. Elsewhere a car park shows as a plain P pin, with its price and opening hours where known.

What does the green P button do?

"Park me now" picks one public car park for you. It looks within 2 km of you, or of the place you searched for, and widens to 10 km if it finds nothing. It skips car parks that are closed, full or customer-only, prefers one with free spaces on a live count, and opens its card so you can start directions.

A car park's details are wrong

Details come from OpenStreetMap volunteers and Irish councils, and can be out of date. Open the car park's card and tap "Report a problem". Always follow the signs at the car park.

I did not get my ticket reminder

Open the iPhone's Settings, then Notifications, then ParkGlas, and switch on Allow Notifications. In ParkGlas, open "Your car" and check the ticket time and the "Remind me" choice.

The map does not show where I am

Open the iPhone's Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Location Services, then ParkGlas, and choose "While Using the App".

How do I see the tour again?

Open Settings in ParkGlas, then About, then "App tour".
